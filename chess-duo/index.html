          01 · Problem

          “Challenge sent”… and then nothing

          On the Chess.com forum, players describe friends shown as offline “even though some of us are in the same room”, challenges that never arrive, and a sender stuck on “Challenge sent”. App reviews add a second theme: too many ads for people who just want a quick game with a friend.

          02 · Ideas

          Status you can trust

          One glass island walks through Sent → Delivered → Seen → Accepted. If a friend is offline, it says so right away and offers a fix.

          A link that always works

          Share a game link on WhatsApp. No friend list, no rating filters, no guessing.

          Quiet while you play

          No ads in game. Clocks and actions float on glass; the board stays solid and readable.

          Opens with the phone

          Folded: the board fills the screen. Unfolded: the board grows and moves and chat appear together below it, without interrupting the game.

          03 · Foldable layout

          Designed around the iPhone Duo’s two displays: 5.4″ outer (1398 × 2034) and 7.6″ inner (1878 × 2670). Both share nearly the same proportions, so the game simply continues when the phone opens: the board grows and the moves and chat panel rises in.

          04 · Glass, used with care

          Inspired by Apple’s Liquid Glass (iOS 26): glass is only for controls that float over content: tab bar, sheet, status island, clocks, result card. The board is solid. With Reduce Transparency the glass turns opaque; with Reduce Motion the morphs become fades.
